Smart Greenhouse Monitoring & Automation with Robotic Plant Disease Detection

Final year project. An IoT greenhouse that monitors temperature, humidity, CO₂, smoke, light, soil moisture and NPK levels, and automatically controls fans, lighting and ventilation windows. A robotic gantry captures plant images, which a deep learning model (EfficientNetB0) analyses to detect diseases early. All data is logged to Firebase and shown in a web app for remote monitoring — reducing manual work and catching disease before it hurts small farmers' yields.
